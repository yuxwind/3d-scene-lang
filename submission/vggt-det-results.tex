\section{VGG-T-Det against SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM, and why VGG-T-Det is dropped}
\label{sec:vggtdet-results}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Both pipelines give the language model a list of \{label, box, score\}. VGG-T-Det is the trained 3D detector
(the released 18-class model fine-tuned to 31 and 65 classes, and test2-hybrid, trained on IndoorDet-65-31k, which
refines and filters SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM proposals and adds its own boxes for objects without a proposal). Every VGG-T-Det variant uses the ground-truth metric scale and the ground-truth pose of the first camera,
in training and at test time. SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM (Section~\ref{sec:sam3}) is zero-shot: no 3D training, no ground truth,
scale estimated from object size priors. Table~\ref{tab:vggtdet-vs-s3} scores both with the same fine-tuned
Qwen3.5-4B models on all 5,130 VSI-Bench questions.

\paragraph{The best detector list is no better than zero-shot SAM~3.}
The best VGG-T-Det result is 70.6 / 63.5 (full / debiased), from test2-hybrid refining the SAM~3 proposals, with the
Exp1 model. The zero-shot SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM list reaches 70.0 / 63.3 with the same model and no ground truth, and
70.4 / 63.7 at the true scale, which is the setting the detector uses. Against that row the detector is $+0.2$ full
and $-0.2$ debiased. Per task the two are nearly identical: counting 76.6 in both, absolute distance 60.9 against 60.8;
SAM~3 is ahead on object size (76.6 against 75.8). The full-score gap comes from route planning ($+1.5$) and room
size ($+0.6$), which the true-scale SAM~3 row answers from the video alone. With the mix-v2-1 model the detector lists
reach 68.6--69.9, against 69.0 for SAM~3 and 69.4 for the video alone. The best detector run is itself a refiner of
SAM~3 proposals rather than a detector on its own.

\paragraph{The detector's boxes are not better either.}
On the same scenes and with the same matching, the cls-31 detector recalls 28.8\% of the ground-truth objects at
IoU $\geq 0.25$ (ScanNet 44.6\%, ARKitScenes 17.2\%, ScanNet++ 16.2\%) and gets the count per label exactly right in 33\%
(too few in 46\%). SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM, on the same 31 classes, recalls 34.1\% at estimated scale and 43.9\% at true scale,
and gets the count exactly right in 47\% (within one in 82\%).

\paragraph{Why VGG-T-Det is dropped.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Fine-tuning did not pay off.} The 63-class detector (20 more epochs from cls-31) was worse than cls-31:
  ScanNet mAP@0.25 on the 31 old classes fell from about 0.53 to 0.467, and it found fewer ground-truth boxes within
  0.5\,m on the 88 VSI-Bench ScanNet scenes (63\% against 73\%). Its 32 new classes reached 0.121 (ScanNet) and 0.082
  (ARKitScenes) mAP@0.25, and every small object stayed at 0.
\item \emph{The architecture misses small objects.} Its queries are farthest-point samples of VGGT's point cloud. In an
  eight-scene memorisation test only 27\% of the ground-truth boxes had a query inside, and VGGT's cloud had no point
  inside 32\% of them (Section~\ref{sec:vggtdet-arch}). More training does not fix this.
\item \emph{The training data needed repeated repair.} ARKitScenes had a wrong origin and a focal length 0.75 times too
  small for portrait videos (1,031 of 2,267 training scenes; Section~\ref{sec:vggtdet-data}), and a uniform box frame for
  ScanNet++ was still open.
\item \emph{It is not video-only.} Every variant needs the ground-truth scale and first-camera pose. SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM
  reaches the same score from the video alone; the estimated scale costs 0.1--0.4 points
  (Sections~\ref{sec:sam3-scale} and~\ref{sec:scale}).
\item \emph{Closed vocabulary.} The cls-31 classes fully cover the objects of only 52.1\% of the VSI-Bench questions.
  SAM~3 is prompted with the names the questions use, including ``room door'' and ``radiator''.
\item \emph{Box geometry is not the bottleneck.} Replacing the SAM~3 centres and sizes by ground truth raises VSI-Bench
  only from 68.6 to 69.9, against 77.3 for the ground-truth list (Section~\ref{sec:sam3}): the errors that matter are
  missing and extra objects. Box regression, the detector's strength, addresses the smaller error.
\item \emph{Refining SAM~3 has a low ceiling.} A detector that only refines and filters SAM~3 proposals can recover at
  most 62--68\% of the ground-truth objects, since it cannot add what SAM~3 missed (Table~\ref{tab:arch-keep}).
\item \emph{Choosing boxes helps more.} With the oraclecnt choice of SAM~3 candidates, letting the code solver answer the
  four geometric tasks raises VSI-Bench from 71.8 to 75.8 (Table~\ref{tab:agent-code-mix}). The open problem is which
  candidates to keep, not how to regress boxes.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Decision.}
After weeks of training, the best VGG-T-Det list reaches 70.6 / 63.5, needs ground-truth scale and pose, and still
depends on SAM~3 for its proposals. Zero-shot SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM reaches 70.0 / 63.3 from the video alone. We drop
VGG-T-Det and work on choosing and merging the SAM~3 + VGGT-SLAM candidates.
