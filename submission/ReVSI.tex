\section{Qwen on ReVSI}
\label{sec:revsi-qwen}

\paragraph{Setup.}
We run the unmodified Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct and Qwen3.5-4B on the ReVSI 64-frame subset (6,616 questions,
380 videos, seven tasks, no appearance order task), video only. Prompts and scoring follow the official
lmms-eval ReVSI task: pre-prompt ``These are frames of a video.'', the VSI-Bench multiple-choice post-prompt, and
``Answer the question using a single integer or decimal number.'' for numeric questions; greedy decoding, at most
16 new tokens, no system prompt, thinking off; the first word of the output is scored by exact match (multiple
choice) or MRA over thresholds $0.50{:}0.95{:}0.05$ (numeric). The question types of a task are averaged and
``Avg.'' is the mean over the tasks. Frames are sampled in two ways: at 1 fps with at most 64 frames, as in our
VSI-Bench evaluation (152 of the 380 videos then get fewer than 64 frames), or all 64 frames that ReVSI provides
(ReVSI's own protocol). The two agree within 1.8 points on every task and within 0.3 on the average.

\paragraph{Results.}
Table~\ref{tab:revsi-qwen} gives the scores. Zero-shot Qwen3.5-4B reaches 45.3 and Qwen3-VL-2B 44.4, far below
Claude Opus~5.5 given the ground-truth scene as text (93.7, Table~\ref{tab:claude-revsi}). The fine-tuned
Qwen3.5-4B (Exp1, \texttt{novggt\_qwen35\_4b\_reason190k\_mixv2\_1\_objq}; 70.4 on VSI-Bench with the
VGGT-SLAM + SAM~3 scene) has not been evaluated on ReVSI yet. ReVSI is a clean test for it: none of the 190k
training rows comes from a ReVSI scene.
