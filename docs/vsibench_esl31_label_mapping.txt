VSI-Bench and Reason-190K labels -> VGG-T-Det 31 classes (ESL31)
================================================================

Used by data/scenelang_vsibench_gt_esl31/build.py (VSI-Bench GT scenes restricted to what the 31-class
detector could output) and, in a wider form, by data/reason190k_videoscene_det/build_mix.py (training data).

Counts are GT boxes over the 288 VSI-Bench scenes (data/scenelang_vsibench_gt_firstcam/rows_all5130.jsonl):
3,197 boxes in 65 labels. 30 labels map to a detector class (2,695 boxes, 84%); 35 labels are dropped (502 boxes).

5. Reason-190K trace labels -> detector classes (data/reason190k_videoscene_det/build_mix.py)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Counts are objects listed in the <scene> blocks of the 145,647 traces (numbers such as "chair 2" stripped):
584,807 objects in 242 labels. 58 labels map to a detector class (430,448 objects, 73.6%);
184 labels are dropped (154,359 objects, 26.4%).
Per dataset, share of listed objects with a detector class: scannet 91.1%, scannetpp 59.8%, arkitscenes 82.1%.
table and desk are interchangeable: a trace "table" takes detector table and desk boxes, and the reverse.
