    absolute distance with all named objects detected: +4 to +15.

F2. VGGT vs VGGT-SLAM 2.0 for object points (oracle 2D boxes; share of named objects within 0.3 m of GT, 4 scenes of 138-434 s):
  plain VGGT, one pass: 64 frames best (7 s, 10 GB); 128 frames no better (18.5 GB); fails on multi-room videos over ~3 min.
  VGGT-SLAM (6-8 GB): default submaps of 16 are unstable (0% on ScanNet scene0645_00); submaps of 32-64 and loop closure
    are needed (70%); higher frame rate helps long ScanNet++ videos (5 fps 64%, 2.5 fps 52%, 1.25 fps 10%).
  windows of 64 frames reconstructed separately are the most reliable (cameras 5-20 cm from GT even on the 434 s video;
    52-79% within 0.3 m when each window is aligned on its own); merging windows is the weak step.
  boxes get larger the more views are merged: IoU >= 0.25 is 60% for one 64-frame pass vs 19-33% after merging 450-600 frames.
